What is this?

A quick lookup for Luigi in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate. Type a character and a stage; the page shows Luigi's zero-to-death (ZTD) window for that character, which combo to use at which percent, and the windows for his other kill confirms.


How do I read the ZTD box?

Down throw, then follow the sequence listed for the opponent's percent. Two kinds of routes are shown, both for DI out (the common DI): the dair → nair → dair routes at low percents, and the short dthrow → (DI guess) dair → uair / wBair / zair / ff → Up B route at higher percents. Windows don't overlap. A gap between windows means that stretch uses a longer route that isn't shown. The full sheet, including those routes and DI in, is in the data.

Tumble percent

"Dthrow tumbles at X%" is the percent where the down throw starts to put them into tumble, with the percents at higher rage underneath (from the Data Compendium). The big ZTD number only counts percents below it: once they tumble, the follow-up depends on whether they DI in or DI out, so it becomes a guess. A "DI guess from here" line in the ZTD list marks where that starts.


Crouch under (blue text)

If the character has a projectile Luigi can crawl under, a blue "Crouch under" line appears right below their name with the list. Data by CrimsonStarfall.


Ledge hang

Moves that hit an opponent hanging on the ledge, colored by difficulty: Easy, Medium, Hard. Moves that don't connect are left out. Labbed with grounded moves only; the foot hitbox on Cyclone and landing dair also work. Skinny characters (Marth, Ganondorf) are easiest to hit at the wrist; big heads and stubby arms (Mario, Kirby) are easiest at the head.


Shield poke

How to hit a shielding opponent through the shield. "Floor" is the route to use; the other rows show how easy it is to poke their front foot, back foot or head. Colors: Easy, Needs spacing, Strict spacing, 2 hits to poke. Parts they can't be poked at are left out. The GIFs showing how to perform these are in the source sheet (about 180 MB, so they aren't bundled here).


wBair → Dtilt followups

Land a weak Bair, turn around Dtilt, then follow up with the listed route (short hop or full hop). It is true on everyone and often leads to a 0-to-death via Fair. These are the most damaging (or killing) followups, with the damage they deal. They're not always optimal because of the precision needed, and many depend on a specific Dtilt → Fair spacing (taller and wider hitboxes work well). Data by lilitimus.


Gameplan

Short matchup notes: Do is what works for Luigi, Watch out is what to avoid or respect, and "Stage" lists stage advice where the notes have it. Compacted from the Luigi Discord matchup notes; only characters with notes have a gameplan.


Forms and variants

Characters with multiple forms get a labelled group (Hero: Accelerate / Oomph, Wii Fit Trainer: Deep Breathing, Mario: Battlefield). The Pokémon Trainer tab isn't labelled in the source; Squirtle / Charizard / Ivysaur were assigned by their Up B kill percents.


Stages

The ZTD windows are not stage-adjusted. The Dthrow → Up B lab lists per-stage ceiling and platform adjustments; those show up under that combo when a stage is selected.


Dtilt combos

Dtilt → Fair and Dtilt → Uair windows come from the "Dtilt Busted" sheets. Rage lowers the required percent by about 1% roughly every 10% of rage starting at 40 (e.g. Bowser: 20% at 0 rage, 19% at 40, 18% at 50). A "…" after a window means the combo might still work at later percents but isn't useful in a match.
